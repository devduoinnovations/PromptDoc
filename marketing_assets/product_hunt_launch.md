## 💬 2. Maker's Launch Comment (First Post at 12:01 AM PST)

Post this immediately after the launch goes live to set up the discussion, invite feedback, and explain the problem you are solving:

```markdown
Hey Product Hunt! 👋 

I'm Safwan, co-founder at **DevDuo Innovation**. 

While building automated AI logic, our engineering team kept running into a frustrating daily bottleneck: we were losing hours copy-pasting active code segments into web browser models during quick debugging sessions, or blowing up token limits by accidentally shipping lockfiles and heavy dependency libraries.

We built **PromptDoc AI** to completely remove that friction. It runs instantly at your root workspace, visualizes directory paths, ignores build/lock/linter cache junk, bundles all active text streams, and saves a highly optimized context payload at **`promptdoc_output/prompt_payload.md`** (as well as copying it straight to your system clipboard) so you can drag-and-drop it into browser LLMs instantly.

To make it even more practical, we added a zero-dependency Gemini API ask-connector with **zero-setup setup**. Just run `promptdoc -a "Your question"`, paste your key once, and get direct, markdown-rendered answers right inside your terminal without opening a browser!

We've packaged this utility and made it 100% open-source and free (Pay-What-You-Want) on Gumroad to give back to the developer community.

We are hanging out in the comment section all day! We'd love to hear:
1. What is your biggest daily headache when managing codebase context with LLMs?
2. What are the must-have features you'd like to see next?

Happy hacking! 💻🚀
```
