# 🚀 Click-by-Click Upload Guide: Gumroad & Product Hunt

This step-by-step walkthrough shows you exactly where to click and what to paste to launch your product in under 5 minutes.

---

## 🏪 Part 1: How to Upload to Gumroad

Follow these exact steps:

### Step 1: Create the Product
1. Log in to [Gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com) and click **Products** on the left menu, then click the pink **New Product** button in the top right.
2. Under **Name of product**, type: 
   `PromptDoc AI: 1-Click Codebase Context & Direct Gemini CLI Chat`
3. Under **Type of product**, select **Digital product** (the first option).
4. Under **Price**, type: `0` (This sets up the free Pay-What-You-Want model).
5. Click **Next: Customize** in the top right.

### Step 2: Customize and Add Marketing Copy
1. In the **Description** box, copy and paste the entire markdown block from **`marketing_assets/gumroad_listing.md`** under *Section 2: Premium Product Page Description*.
2. Under **URL**, customize your unique link path, e.g., `promptdoc-ai` or `devduo-promptdoc`.
3. Under **Cover**, click **Upload files** and select your locally generated cover image:
   📁 **`marketing_assets/gumroad_cover.png`**
4. Under **Product Info / Call to action**, select **Get** or **Buy this**.

### Step 3: Upload the Code Package
1. Scroll down to **Content**.
2. Click **Upload files** (or the computer icon).
3. Select your repository folder or zip your code files and upload them. (Alternatively, you can just paste your GitHub repository link as a redirect URL under **Redirect to an external URL** in the settings so they grab it straight from GitHub!).
4. Click **Save and continue** in the top right.

### Step 4: Publish & Go Live!
1. Under the **Publish** tab, click the pink **Publish** button.
2. Copy your product link! You are officially live on Gumroad!

## 🐱 Part 2: How to Upload to Product Hunt

Follow these exact steps:

### Step 1: Start the Post
1. Log in to [ProductHunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com) and click the **Submit** button in the top right corner, then select **Product**.
2. Paste your Gumroad product link or GitHub repository link under **Product URL** and click **Next**.

### Step 2: Fill in Details
1. Under **Name**, type: `PromptDoc AI`
2. Under **Tagline**, paste: `Direct Gemini CLI Chat & 1-Click Codebase Context`
3. Under **Description**, write a short summary: 
   `Stop wasting hours copy-pasting code fragments. Scan your root directory, visual-map structure, auto-ignore caches/builds, and run direct Gemini CLI chat queries natively from your console in 1-click.`
4. Under **Topics / Categories**, search and add: `Developer Tools`, `Artificial Intelligence`, and `Open Source`.

### Step 3: Upload Visual Assets
1. Under **Logo**, upload a small square icon.
2. Under **Gallery / Media**, drag and drop your locally generated Product Hunt thumbnail image:
   📁 **`marketing_assets/product_hunt_cover.png`**

### Step 4: Launch and Post Maker's Comment
1. Scroll down and click **Launch Now**.
2. Immediately go to the newly created listing's comment section.
3. Paste the entire maker's launch comment from **`marketing_assets/product_hunt_launch.md`** under *Section 2: Maker's Launch Comment*.
4. Pin your comment to the top!

## ✉️ Part 3: Set up your automated Lead Capture Email in Gumroad

To gather email lists for **Sagenta**:
1. In your Gumroad dashboard, click **Workflows** in the left menu, then click **New Workflow**.
2. Name the workflow: `PromptDoc Welcome & Waitlist Capture`.
3. Set the trigger to: **After purchase of: PromptDoc AI** (or send to all buyers).
4. Set the timing to: **1 minute** after purchase.
5. In the email subject, type: `Welcome to PromptDoc AI + Sagenta Private Beta 🚀`
6. In the email body, copy and paste the email copy from **`marketing_assets/gumroad_listing.md`** under *Section 3: Post-Purchase Automated Email Sequence*.
7. Click **Save and activate**. Gumroad will now automatically harvest email waitlist signups for you on autopilot!
